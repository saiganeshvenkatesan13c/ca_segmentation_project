# Optimizing deep learning–based cell segmentation for calcium imaging analysis
Calcium imaging segmentation project undertaken from Feb 2026 - Apr 2026 at the [Kirkeby Lab](https://renew.ku.dk/research/kirkeby_group/),  [Novo Nordisk Foundation Center for Stem Cell Medicine, reNEW](https://renew.ku.dk/).


Bioinformatics Project 1 (7.5 ECTS) carried out as a part of M.Sc Bioinformatics at the University of Copenhagen.
